The Ondine way

“The best ingredient
is attention.”

Chef Inês Duarte grew up between Setúbal’s fish market and her grandmother’s tiled kitchen. At Ondine, she keeps that memory close: buy less, buy better, and let the ingredient speak before the chef does.

The result is a menu that moves like water — raw, charred, briny, warm. No theatre for theatre’s sake. Just the rare pleasure of being looked after.

Inês DuarteChef & founder
